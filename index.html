Samsung and the Springboks, eleven months in: has it paid?

Two-year deal, five squads, no fee disclosed. Samsung's South African shipments grew 15% in a falling market during the first three quarters. Correlation or return? INANI reads the record.

Sources: SuperSport · Omdia

A footballer called TLB sells backhoe loaders. That is a measurable deal.

Barloworld Equipment built a Cat 426 campaign around Mbokazi's nickname, with a phone number and a monthly price on the creative. Every enquiry on 0860 BARLOW during the flight is attributable. INANI's kind of sponsorship.

Source: @barloworldequipmentsouthafrica · @qt_sports

LIBBO2: a Springbok flyhalf whose deal is already tracked to the till

Libbie Janse van Rensburg's Boerboel Wear code puts her name on every order it drives. With 10.5k followers she is the size of athlete the market ignores and the exact case INANI prices: a code, a conversion rate, a commission.

Thomas du Toit drives off in a BAIC B30

A vehicle sponsorship, not a cash deal: the Springbok prop gets the locally assembled B30 and BAIC gets first-hand social content on it. The kind of in-kind partnership INANI values at the car's retail price plus the content.
