One to watch · 2027

Jake Dave: the SACS 10 who scored 24 against Paul Roos in Grade 11

A try, two conversions and five penalties in a 38-34 loss, then the game management that beat Wynberg 44-29. He came up through WP's Grant Khomo u16A side with centre James Sale, and with Kwakanya Gobe they lead a group that has played together since u13 and reaches matric in 2027. The first valuation INANI publishes before a contract exists.

Sources: SuperSport Schools, 26 Jul 2026 · SA Rugby magazine, 23 Aug 2026 · SuperSport Schools, Feb 2026 · Instagram
