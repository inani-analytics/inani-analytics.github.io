Jake Dave, and the SACS side that grew up together.

Twenty-four of SACS's thirty-four points against Paul Roos came off the boot and the feet of a Grade 11 flyhalf: a try, two conversions and five penalties in a 38-34 loss that NovaNews called the performance of the weekend. Four weeks later he managed the game that beat Wynberg 44-29. Dave came up through Western Province's Grant Khomo u16A side with centre James Sale, and with Kwakanya Gobe they anchor a group that has played together since u13 and reaches matric in 2027. This is the roster INANI is built for: a number on the player before any professional body holds his image rights.

Insight · Sponsorship

Banyana Banyana: Africa's champions of 2022 spent four months without a sponsor.

Sasol has backed the women's national team since 2009. The deal lapsed on 30 June 2025, two days after the squad sat out training in Morocco over unpaid match fees, and five days before WAFCON kicked off. South Africa finished fourth and collected $300,000. The renewal came on 24 October 2025, four more years to 2029, value undisclosed. The most successful women's team in the country went into a continental tournament with nothing on the training kit, and nobody published what the shirt was worth. That is the number INANI puts on the table before the next renewal.

Insight · Sponsorship review

Samsung and the Springboks, eleven months in. Has it paid?

On 27 November 2025 Samsung became official mobile and consumer electronics partner to SA Rugby's five national squads and an associate sponsor of the Vodacom URC, on a two-year term with no fee disclosed. Here is what the public record can and cannot say about the return.

Signed27 Nov 2025SA Rugby and URC, two years
Squads covered5Boks, Bok Women, Juniors, Sevens men and women
FeeUndisclosedno filing, no reported figure
Players activated6+S26 Ultra unboxing, May 2026

What has been activated

The Galaxy S26 Ultra unboxing in May 2026 put Handré Pollard, Willie le Roux, Ruan Nortje, Marco van Staden, Kurt-Lee Arendse and Canan Moodie in front of the camera with the S26 Ultra, Buds4 Pro and Watch Ultra. Samsung's stated plan covers stadium activations, digital content and fan competitions; the squads also receive devices and training-facility electronics.

Sources: SuperSport, 27 Nov 2025 · Lifestyle and Tech, 15 May 2026

What the market did

South Africa smartphone shipments, Q2 2026 v Q2 2025Omdia, via SammyFans

StatCounter puts Samsung at 49.75% of South African mobile web traffic in September 2026, ahead of Apple at 18.57% and Honor at 8.8%.

Sources: Omdia Q2 2026 · StatCounter, Sep 2026

INANI's reading

Correlation, not attribution. Samsung grew 15% in a falling market during the first three full quarters of the deal. That is consistent with the sponsorship working; it does not prove it. Price moves, the S26 launch cycle and Transsion's retreat all sit in the same quarter.

What would settle it. Tracked codes on Springbok content, connected-account reach for the six activated players, and a sales lift test against the URC weeks. None of that is public, so the honest answer on return is: unmeasured, with the market signal pointing the right way.

What the renewal needs. With the two-year term ending in late 2027, Samsung has one URC season and one Rugby Championship left to turn the signal into evidence. INANI's benchmark is ready when the fee is on the table.

Mofokeng to Union SG: a €3m fee for a World Cup starter.

Relebohile Mofokeng left Orlando Pirates for Royale Union Saint-Gilloise on a four-year deal straight after the 2026 World Cup, where he started at 10 against South Korea in the win that put Bafana into the knockouts for the first time. The fee is reported at about €3m (R55m to R60m). INANI prices the sale against the player's record, not the fee the buyer chose to disclose.

Two 2026 entries, one reading

Makhanya, £3m to Rangers. Philadelphia Union bought him from Stellenbosch and sold him on at 23. The profit went to MLS, not the PSL.

Mofokeng, €3m to Union SG. Pirates sold a 21-year-old World Cup starter at the price of a mid-table Belgian winger. The market repriced him within four months.

The INANI point. South African clubs are selling at the bottom of the band because nobody puts a number on the player before the buyer does. A valuation on the table before the window opens is the difference between €3m and €6m.
