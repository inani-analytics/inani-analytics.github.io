About INANI

Inani is isiXhosa for number, value, amount. INANI puts one on sport.

Why INANI exists

The tools that measure the sports market were built for leagues that could already afford them. Everyone else negotiates on instinct: the school leaver signing a first contract, the semi-professional with a sponsor and no number, the retired international whose name is still worth more than anyone has told her.

INANI puts a sourced value on athletes, teams and commercial rights, and shows its working. Every figure on the site comes from a named source; estimates are marked as estimates.
