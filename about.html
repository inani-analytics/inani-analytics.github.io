About INANI

Inani is isiXhosa for number, value, amount. The company exists to put one on sport.

Why INANI exists

Sport is a global asset class. Athletes, teams, leagues and rights are bought, sold and sponsored across borders every day, and the tools that measure that market were built for the leagues that could already afford them. Everyone else negotiates on instinct: the school leaver signing a first contract, the semi-professional with a real following and no price, the rights holder renewing a sponsorship without a benchmark, the brand paying a fee it cannot defend.

INANI closes that gap. We bring sports, media and investment data into one place, put a sourced number on athletes, teams, leagues and commercial rights, and show our working. Our mission is verified value for every athlete and property that has been measured by nobody.

Know the numbers. Understand the value. Put a number on it.

How a figure gets onto INANI, and what we will not do to get one.

How a value is built

Every record starts with a primary source: a club or union announcement, a filed deal, a connected social account, a document the athlete uploads. We verify it, normalise it to the currency the deal was done in, and benchmark it against comparable properties by sport, tier and territory.

Reported versus estimated

A reported figure is one a named source published. An estimate is INANI's model output, or a media estimate we carry with attribution. Estimates are always labelled EST with a dashed treatment and are never presented as fact. Where no figure exists, the board says Undisclosed.

Reach is measured, never guessed

Follower and engagement numbers come from connected accounts or dated public reports. INANI does not model reach for an athlete who has not connected their accounts.
