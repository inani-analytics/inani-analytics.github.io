From fee to revenue.

Every INANI partnership carries a tracked route to the till: a promo code, a tagged link, an affiliate page or a retailer sales feed. The report shows what the brand paid, what it sold, and what it earned on the spend. Media value is reported too, as the secondary measure it is.
